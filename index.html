TWO MAPS. DIFFERENT MEANINGS.

Look closely. Ask one more question.

The Bloch sphere represents one-qubit states; its ghosts compare separate preparations. The complex plane represents numbers with real and imaginary coordinates. A point on that plane is not a complete qubit state.
